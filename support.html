Switch a permission on

Say It Here needs up to three permissions. If one is off, open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, and open the list named below. Switch Say It Here on, then reopen the app if it asks you to.

  • Microphone: so it can hear you.
  • Input Monitoring: so it can notice your trigger key.
  • Accessibility: optional. It lets the app paste your words into the app you are using. Without it, the words are copied and you press Command-V.

The trigger key does not work

Open the Say It Here icon in the menu bar, choose Settings, and pick another trigger key.

Usage counts and opening at login

Both are switches in Settings, in the menu-bar menu. Usage counts are on by default and nothing is sent until you finish setup; you can switch them off on the last setup screen or here. See the privacy policy for what the usage counts contain.

The larger voice model

Say It Here works with the voice model built into it. After setup it downloads a larger one in the background (1.6 GB). It waits for a normal connection, not a hotspot or a low-data one, and it does not start if the disk is nearly full. To stop it or start it, open the menu-bar menu.

The free trial and the unlock

You can use Say It Here free for 7 days. The 7 days start the first time Say It Here runs on your Mac, each Mac has its own 7 days, and the menu and Settings show how many days are left. After that, dictation pauses until you unlock it once. The unlock is a one-time purchase through the App Store, with no subscription. To get it back on a new Mac or after a reinstall, use the Restore Purchase button in the app.

Which Macs it runs on

Macs with Apple silicon, on macOS 26 or later.

Delete your usage counts

Settings shows your install ID with a Copy button. Email that ID to fitshala.ai@gmail.com and we delete the counts linked to it. The app has no account, so there is nothing else to delete.
