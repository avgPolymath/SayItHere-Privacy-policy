Privacy Policy for Say It Here

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Say It Here is a dictation app for the Mac. This page says what the app does with your information. It is short because the app collects very little.

1. Your voice and your words stay on your Mac

  • Your voice and the words you dictate are turned into text on your Mac. We never upload or store them.
  • There is no account and the app does not ask for your name or email.

2. Anonymous usage counts

The app sends anonymous usage counts, to help make it better. They are on when you first open the app. Nothing is sent until you finish setup, and on the last setup screen you can switch them off before anything leaves your Mac. You can switch them off at any time in Settings, in the menu-bar menu.

  • Random install ID: each install gets a random ID. It is not linked to your name, your email or your Apple ID.
  • What a count contains: which setup screens you viewed, and whether setup was paused, resumed or finished and how many seconds it took; whether each permission was granted and how many seconds it took, and if one was switched off later; which trigger key you chose; whether text is typed into the other app or copied; how many free days you had left when the trial screen showed; whether you opened Settings or the repair window; the open-at-login switch, and the usage-count switch when you turn it back on; whether the larger voice model finished downloading. For your first dictation in another app, whether it was typed or copied. For each dictation, only the outcome (typed, copied, cancelled or no sound), the seconds until it was ready and the seconds of speech. Every count holds only numbers, on or off, or a word from a short fixed list. There is no free text.
  • Never in a count: your audio, your words, what you type, or which app you were using.
  • Where the counts go: to PostHog, a product analytics service, hosted in the United States. They are kept for 12 months. PostHog's servers receive the IP address each count is sent from, but it is not stored with the count and no location is worked out from it.

3. Deleting your counts

Settings shows your install ID with a Copy button. Email that ID to fitshala.ai@gmail.com and we delete the counts linked to it.

4. The larger voice model

The app works with the voice model built into it. After setup, it also downloads a larger voice model from Hugging Face in the background (1.6 GB). It waits for a normal connection, not a hotspot or a low-data one, and it does not start if the disk is nearly full. You can stop it in the menu bar, and you can start it yourself there at any time. Hugging Face, which hosts the file, can see your IP address and the time of the download. We do not receive that information.

5. Payment

The one-time unlock is an in-app purchase handled by Apple. We never see your card details.
